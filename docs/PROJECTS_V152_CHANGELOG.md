# Projects V15.2 Changelog

## Muc tieu

V15.2 sua cac diem con lech trong V15.1: nut them bi trung, form noi keo theo noi dung phu, editor mat che do dinh dang sau khi bam toolbar, va bo sung form noi cho Ke hoach ngay / Mon hoc.

## Thay doi chinh

- Hoc tap:
  - Bo nut `Ghi chu nhanh` vi trung voi `Them muc hoc tap`.
  - Khi form them/sua dang noi, an panel Pomodoro/Focus Mode de nguoi dung chi thay form can nhap.
- Ho tro ghi bai:
  - Sua toolbar de in dam, in nghieng, gach chan, gach ngang duy tri lien tuc khi go tiep.
  - Nut dinh dang co trang thai active va bam lai de tat.
  - Them tuy chon so hang / so cot truoc khi chen bang.
  - O trong bang co the keo chinh kich thuoc trong editor.
- Nhat ky:
  - Sua chon mau chu de mau tiep tuc ap dung khi go tiep.
  - Bam lai mau dang chon se tat ve mau chu mac dinh theo nen hien tai.
  - Bang mau khong tu dong dong sau moi lan chon mau.
- Ke hoach ngay va Mon hoc:
  - Them entry mode noi cho form them/sua, khong phai keo xuong cot phu.
- Editor dung chung:
  - Giu selection/caret khi bam cac nut dinh dang co ban.
