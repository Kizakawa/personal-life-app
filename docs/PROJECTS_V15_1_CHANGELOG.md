## Thay doi chinh

- Hoc tap:
  - Nut `Them muc hoc tap` va `Ghi chu nhanh` mo man nhap ngay truoc mat.
  - Sau khi luu muc moi, he thong chon dung muc vua tao.
- On thi:
  - Nut `Them muc on thi` mo form noi ngay tren man hinh.
  - Sua loi `Tao ke hoach mon nay` do gui ngay rong len backend.
  - Sau khi luu checklist moi, he thong chon dung checklist vua tao.
- Kho tri thuc, Tai lieu, Du an ca nhan:
  - Nut them/sua mo form theo kieu entry screen noi, khong con phai keo xuong cuoi trang.
- Ho tro ghi bai:
  - Doi sang giao dien soan thao kieu trang vo/Docs.
  - Them thanh cong cu: can trai, giua, phai, in dam, in nghieng, gach chan, gach ngang, co chu, chen bang.
  - Them nut chup/tai anh vo viet de AI trich chu va dua vao bai ghi.
